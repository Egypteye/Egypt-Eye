# The two-brand split: auditing the 34 Egypt Eye articles

Decision of 20 November 2026: **Flying Dresses Egypt stays independent as the
specialist authority. No migration.** Egypt Eye Travel remains the broad
travel brand. This document applies that rule to the 36 overlapping articles
(34 live, 2 archived) one at a time.

Judged on **what each article is actually for**, not on whether it names a
destination Flying Dresses also uses. Per the instruction: a topic is not
excluded merely because Egypt Eye mentions the same place.

---

## The dividing line, as I am applying it

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is the subject the flying dress itself — the garment, the throw, the colours, the hire? | **Flying Dresses** |
| Is it a flying dress session: what happens, what to wear under it, how to pose, where it flies best? | **Flying Dresses** |
| Is it "a photoshoot at the pyramids" as a general service — hiring a photographer, horse and camel, family portraits? | **Egypt Eye.** This is Egypt Eye's own product, not ours |
| Is it a destination, an itinerary, a tour, a transfer, a cultural guide? | **Egypt Eye** |
| Is it an occasion *celebrated with our dresses* — proposal, birthday, reveal, anniversary? | **Flying Dresses** |

The useful distinction that falls out: **"pyramids photoshoot" is Egypt Eye's.
"Flying dress photoshoot at the pyramids" is ours.** Both can exist. They are
different products with different buyers.

### What "narrow it" means, and why it is not migration

You ruled out moving these, and that holds. But two sites owned by one
business, both targeting "flying dress photoshoot Egypt", still compete
whatever the intent behind them — Google does not know they share an owner.

The resolution that honours "no migration" is to **edit the Egypt Eye version
so the two articles stop wanting the same visitor**:

- Egypt Eye's version becomes the **trip-planner's** view: where a flying
  dress session fits in a Cairo itinerary, how to book one around a tour, how
  much of a day to allow. It keeps its URL, its history and its rankings.
- Flying Dresses writes the **practitioner's** view at real depth: how the
  throw works, the thirteen colours against limestone, what happens minute by
  minute, which of the five locations suits which photograph.
- Each links to the other once, with descriptive anchor text.

That is an edit to five Egypt Eye articles. It is far less risky than a
migration and it is the only version of "keep both" that does not leave them
bidding against each other.

**If you would rather not touch Egypt Eye at all**, the fallback is that
Flying Dresses writes only at angles those five do not occupy. That is
workable but it permanently concedes the strongest keyword in the category to
the travel brand, which is the opposite of the positioning you just chose.
