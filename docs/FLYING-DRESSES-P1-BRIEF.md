# The P1 batch, in commercial order

20 November 2026. For approval. **Nothing here is published.**

You asked for the strongest commercial opportunities first, not the easiest
articles to write. So this list is **reordered from the content map**, which
was grouped by cluster. The map's clusters are still the right structure; this
is the sequence to build them in.

---

## How I ranked these

Four questions, in this order:

1. **Does a sale sit directly behind it?** A reader who finishes convinced
   should be one click from a page that takes an enquiry.
2. **Is the commercial page currently undefended?** Several of our own
   commercial pages have no editorial support at all. Those are worth more
   than a tenth article about the rooftop.
3. **Is anyone else holding the ground?** Where Egypt Eye already ranks, our
   article competes with our own business until the pillar edit happens.
4. **Does the photography actually exist?** Every item below is illustrated
   from frames already in the Drive. None needs a shoot.

"Easy to write" is not on that list, and two of the easiest pieces in the map
have dropped down it.

---

## Tier 1 — the thirteen wardrobe pages

**These outrank every article, and it is not close.**

They are the only commercial pages in this programme that do not exist yet,
they sell the one thing only this brand has, Egypt Eye has never written a
word about dresses, and there are 106+ photographs across thirteen named
garments waiting to fill them. Every colour article in Tier 3 is worth
roughly double once these exist, because the articles would have somewhere to
send people.
