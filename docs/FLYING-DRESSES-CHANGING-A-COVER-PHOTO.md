# Changing a photoshoot's cover photograph yourself

The cover is the picture on a shoot's card on the home page and the
Experiences list, and the big one at the top of that shoot's own page. All
three are the same photograph, so you change it once.

You do this in the Studio, in a browser. Nothing here needs a developer.

## Where it is

1. Go to `/studio` on flyingdressesegypt.com and sign in.
2. Open **Flying Dresses — Photoshoot** and pick the shoot.
3. The first group of fields is **The shoot**. **Cover photograph** is in there.

## Uploading, replacing, removing

- **Upload a new one**: drag the file onto the field, or press **Upload**.
- **Use one already uploaded**: press **Select** and pick it from your media.
- **Replace**: upload or select a different file. The old one stays in your
  media library, so nothing is lost and you can put it back.
- **Remove**: the **X** on the field. The shoot then goes back to the
  photograph it had before, which is the one built into the site. It does not
  go blank.

You see the picture in the field as soon as it uploads. That is the preview.
Nothing is live until you press **Publish**, and the button stays orange while
there are unpublished changes.

## Each shoot is its own

Changing the White Desert cover changes the White Desert cover. It cannot
touch Fayoum, the dunes, the rooftop or the yacht, and it does not touch the
gallery lower down that shoot's page. Those are separate.

## Any shape is fine

Portrait, landscape or square. The card takes the shape of whatever you put
in, rather than cropping the picture to fit a shape it was not. This is the
change made on 8 November 2026 after covers were losing heads and dress
trains: they were not badly cropped, they were being forced into a wide box
that no upright photograph fits.

So you do not need to crop anything before uploading. Upload the frame you
want shown.

## The circle on the picture

Click the cover, then **Edit**, and you can drag a circle around on it. That
is the focal point, and it only matters in the few places where the space is
a fixed shape and something genuinely has to be cut. Put it on the face, or
on the dress. Most of the site shows the whole picture and ignores it.

## Describe the photograph

The field under the cover. Write what is happening in it, for someone who
cannot see it and for Google: "A gold flying dress on the white chalk
formations at sunset", never "IMG_4821". If you leave it empty the shoot's
previous description is used, which is better than nothing but is describing
a different picture.

## If a change does not show

In order, the usual causes:

1. It is saved as a draft. The **Publish** button is still orange.
2. The page is cached. Press **Refresh the site from Sanity** on
   `/admin/paypal`.
3. You changed the cover on a different shoot than the one you are looking at.

## What it does not change

The credit line under a photograph. Those belong to specific files the agency
supplied and are recorded against them; a cover you upload carries no credit
rather than inheriting the old picture's, because that sentence describes a
different photograph and printing it here would attribute one picture's
history to another.
