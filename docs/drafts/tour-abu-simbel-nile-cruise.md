## Day by day

**Day 1 — Arrival in Cairo**
Met inside the airport, not outside it: VIP meet and assist handles the queue
and the paperwork, and you are walked out to a private car. Transfer to your
hotel, check in, and nothing else asked of you.

**Day 2 — Giza: the Pyramids, the Sphinx and a camera**
Collected from the hotel for the Giza plateau. A camel or a horse, whichever
you prefer, and a professional photoshoot at the pyramids. The Great Sphinx. A stop at an
oils and essence factory. Lunch, then back to the hotel.

**Day 3 — Old Cairo and the market**
The Coptic quarter and its churches, then the National Museum of Egyptian
Civilization. Lunch. The afternoon is Khan el-Khalili, the old market. Back to the hotel.

**Day 4 — South to Aswan, and aboard**
Transfer to Cairo airport and fly to Aswan, where a car is waiting. Check in to
the cruise and have lunch on board. Then Philae Temple on its island, the High
Dam, and a view of the Unfinished Obelisk — abandoned in the quarry when it
split, still attached to the bedrock. Dinner and the night in Aswan.

**Day 5 — Abu Simbel before sunrise, then the river**
Leave at about four in the morning for the drive south to Abu Simbel. The
temples of Ramses II and of Nefertari, at the hour when the light is still low
on the facade and the coaches have not arrived. Back to the boat for lunch,
and the cruise begins sailing. Kom Ombo when the boat docks — self-guided, and
optional. Dinner and the night on board.

**Day 6 — Edfu, the Esna lock, and Luxor's East Bank**
Breakfast on board while the boat keeps moving. Edfu Temple at the cruise
stop — again self-guided and optional. Through the Esna lock, which is worth
being on deck for, and into Luxor. The East Bank in the afternoon: Karnak,
then Luxor Temple. Dinner and the night in Luxor.

**Day 7 — The West Bank, then back to Cairo**
Breakfast on board and check out. Across to the West Bank for the Valley of the
Kings, the terraces of Hatshepsut's temple at Deir el-Bahari, and the Colossi
of Memnon. Transfer to Luxor airport and fly to Cairo, where a car meets you
and takes you to your hotel.

**Day 8 — Departure**
Collected from the hotel and taken to Cairo airport for your flight home.
