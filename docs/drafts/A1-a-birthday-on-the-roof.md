## A birthday on the roof, with the dress

A birthday here is not a party we put on. It is a photoshoot built around the
person whose birthday it is, on a rooftop above Giza, and whatever else you
want around it is arranged on top of that.

Worth saying plainly at the start, because the two words that cause the most
confusion in this category are "included" and "arranged", and they mean
different things on your quotation.

### What the two we have photographed were

Both of them were a flying dress session with a cake in it. Both wore
[Cherry](/wardrobe/cherry), the deep red, which is the most requested colour
we run.

The first was shot in soft haze, the red train laid out the full width of the
deck and across the cushioned bench, two pyramids pale behind. She holds a
small cake with a gold topper on it. In one frame she is putting on a small
gold crown, which was hers rather than ours, and it is the best photograph of
the set.

The second was a brighter evening, a pyramid clear behind her, and the thing
that makes it is that she is not alone in it. Two other people are in several
of the frames, crouched either side of her while she holds a white cake with
red hearts piped down it. Those are the photographs people keep from a
birthday: not the portrait, the three of them.

### Here is what those photographs do not show

**Neither of them had a built decoration setup.** No arch, no signage, no
balloons. Just the roof as it stands, the dress, the cake and the people.

That is worth being straight about, because the page you are reading this from
offers decoration as something we arrange, and we do not currently have a
photograph of a decorated birthday on this roof to show you. We have
photographs of decorated proposals and a decorated gender reveal, and they are
the same craft pointed at a different occasion, but they are not birthdays and
we are not going to caption them as if they were.

Ask us what is available for your date and we will send you pictures of the
actual setups rather than leave you to guess from these.

### Included, arranged, quoted

The distinction matters more here than anywhere else on this site.

**Included.** The rooftop, confirmed with the venue for your date and your
hour before anything is agreed. The dress from the collection in the colour
you choose. The direction, so nobody has to know how to pose. The photography
of the evening itself: the people, the cake, the moment everyone sings, and
portraits of whoever the evening is for.

**Arranged, and priced in your quotation.** The decoration. A setup from the
designs we run, pointed at what you are celebrating. You choose from what is
available for your date and what you choose is priced with the rest, rather
than being folded into a headline figure.

**Through a supplier.** The cake. It comes from a baker we work with rather
than our own kitchen, so the design, what is possible and what it costs are
confirmed with you before anything is ordered. We do not publish flavours,
sizes, dietary options, a price or a cut-off date for ordering, because none
of those has been set.

### How it is priced

A photoshoot is priced per person. A party is the rooftop by the hour, with
the photography quoted on top of it.

Which of those a birthday is depends on what you are actually planning. Three
people and a cake is a photoshoot. Fifteen people and an evening is the roof
by the hour. Tell us roughly how many are coming and we will tell you which
one you are looking at before you have to commit to anything.

We do not publish a minimum booking, a deposit, an overtime rate or a
cancellation policy for this service, because none has been set. You get the
terms for your booking in writing with the quotation.

### Bringing people

You can add companions to the booking, and the second of the two sessions
above is the argument for it.

Tell us the number when you enquire, because capacity is the venue's to set
rather than ours and it has to be confirmed with them for your date.

### Afterwards

The photographs are colour-graded and delivered after the session.

We do not publish a number or a delivery deadline for a birthday, because
neither has been set for this service. You get both in writing for your
booking. A figure on a web page that turns out not to fit your evening is
worse than no figure at all.

## FAQs

**Is the decoration included?**
No, and this is the one worth reading twice. For a birthday the decoration is
arranged by us and priced in your quotation. It is included in some of our
other services, which is exactly why it is spelled out here.

**Can you organise a cake?**
Yes, through a baker we work with. The design, what is possible and the cost
are confirmed with you before anything is ordered. We do not publish flavours,
sizes or a price because none has been set.

**How much does a birthday on the roof cost?**
It depends on whether you are booking a photoshoot or an evening. A photoshoot
is priced per person; the rooftop as a party is by the hour with photography
quoted on top. Tell us how many people are coming and we will tell you which
one applies.

**Can other people come?**
Yes. Tell us the number when you enquire so it can be confirmed with the
venue, because capacity is theirs to set.

**Which dress should I pick for a birthday?**
Both of the birthdays we have photographed used Cherry, the deep red, and it
is the most requested colour in the collection. Any of the thirteen is
included at the same price, so the choice is about the photograph you want.

**How many photographs do we get, and when?**
We do not publish a figure or a deadline for this service, because neither has
been set. You get both in writing with your quotation.

mistake.
