engaged"**. I think that title is wrong, for two reasons.

and what it costs."** It carries the keyword, it is honest about the
photography, and the cost section is the part Egypt Eye's general articles

## An anniversary photoshoot in Egypt: the evening, the roof and what it costs

Alessandra and Gianluca have a tradition. Every anniversary they travel
somewhere they have not been, and they photograph it in their wedding clothes.
In September 2026 that was a rooftop above Giza, and the photographs on this
page are from that evening.

It is the only anniversary session in our library, which means this article is
describing one real evening rather than averaging a category.

### What the roof actually is

A rooftop in Giza with the pyramids across the skyline, confirmed with the
venue for your date and your hour. The pyramids are the view from it. They are
not a backdrop we have placed you in front of, and between you and them are
the roofs of the town, which are in the wide frames because they are really
there.

The corner used for couples is a low built-in bench run with patterned
cushions and kilim rugs over a wooden deck. It photographs as somewhere to sit
rather than a set, which is most of why it works for two people. You can lean
on it, sit on it, or walk the length of the deck with the plateau behind you.

### Golden hour is the whole product

Every usable frame from that September evening is within about an hour of
sunset.

The light at that hour does something specific here that matters more than it
would elsewhere: the haze over the plateau turns the pyramids pale and slightly
unreal while leaving the people in front of them warm and solid. You get
separation without anything being lit. Shoot the same spot at noon and the
pyramids go flat and grey and the couple squint.

So the hour is not a preference. It is the thing you are booking.

### Two prices, and the difference between them

A session here is priced per person, so a couple is two.

- **A flying dress session, $199 per person.** A dress from the collection in
  the colour you choose, an assistant throwing the fabric, and the directed
  session that goes with it.
- **A regular session, $100 per person.** The same rooftop and the same
  direction, in your own clothes.

Alessandra and Gianluca took the second option, because the entire point of
their tradition is the clothes they already own. Her lace wedding dress and his
black tuxedo are the story; a flying dress would have replaced it.

That is worth saying plainly, because the cheaper option is the right one for a
significant number of anniversaries and nobody selling you a photoshoot
usually says so.

**Decoration is included in both.** A setup from the designs we are running on
your date, part of what you are paying rather than an extra on top. Tell us the
mood and we will send you what is available.

One honest caveat about the photographs on this page: that session did not use
decoration. The frames show the roof and the couple, nothing staged. If
decoration matters to you, ask to see what is running for your date rather than
reading it off these pictures.

### You do not have to know how to pose

The most common worry about a couple shoot, and the easiest to solve.

You are told where to stand, which way to turn, and what to do with your hands.
The frames people like best from that evening are not the formal ones. They are
the two of them laughing at something off camera, him in shirtsleeves and a bow
tie with his sunglasses on, her holding a phone out at arm's length taking a
selfie in the middle of a professional shoot. Those happen because somebody is
directing the session loosely enough to let them.

### If it is a surprise, nothing reaches your partner

Planned the same way a proposal is, because the risk is identical: one message
to the wrong place and the evening is no longer a surprise.

You pick the channel, email or WhatsApp or whichever you choose, and we use
only that one. Nothing goes anywhere you have not named. Tell us when you
enquire if the address you are writing from is one you share. The setup is
ready before you arrive, so the first time they see it is the moment you
wanted, rather than while somebody is still tying a balloon.

### How the evening is arranged

1. Tell us the date and what it is for. An anniversary, a honeymoon, or just
   an evening that deserves more than a restaurant. Say whether it is a
   surprise.
2. Choose the session. Flying dress or your own clothes. The difference is the
   rate per person and what the photographs look like.
3. Choose how it looks. We send you the decoration designs available for your
   date. It is included, so this is a choice rather than an upsell.
4. We confirm the roof. The venue, the date and the hour are agreed, and the
   quotation comes back to you in writing. Nothing is held before that.
5. The evening, photographed. Directed throughout.

### Afterwards

The photographs are colour-graded and delivered after the session.

We do not publish a number of photographs or a delivery deadline for this
service, because neither has been set. You get both in writing for your
booking. A count on a web page that turns out not to fit your evening is worse
than no count at all.

### What you can add

A cinematic couple's film, shot and edited in house and quoted for your
session. A flying dress with a coordinated outfit for him, if you want the
pictures to be the other kind of anniversary photograph.

## FAQs

**What is the best time of day?**
Within about an hour of sunset. It is not a preference, it is what makes the
pyramids separate from the people in front of them. At midday the plateau goes
flat and grey.

**How much does an anniversary photoshoot cost?**
It is priced per person, so a couple is two. $199 per person for a flying dress
session, $100 per person for a regular session in your own clothes. Decoration
is included in both.

**Do we need to bring special clothes?**
No. A regular session is explicitly in your own clothes, and it is the right
choice if what you are wearing is part of the point. Alessandra and Gianluca
wore the clothes they were married in.

**Neither of us can pose. Is that a problem?**
No, and it is the most common thing people say before a couple shoot. You are
directed throughout: where to stand, which way to turn, what to do with your
hands.

**Can you keep it secret from my partner?**
Yes. You name one channel and we use only that one. Tell us if the address you
are writing from is shared.

**How many photographs do we get, and when?**
We do not publish a figure or a deadline for this service, because neither has
been set. You get both in writing with your quotation rather than a number here
that may not fit your booking.
