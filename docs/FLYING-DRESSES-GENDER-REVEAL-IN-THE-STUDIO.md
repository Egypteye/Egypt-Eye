# Editing the Gender reveal page

**flyingdressesegypt.com/studio → ✿ Flying Dresses — Gender reveal page**

Already filled in with what the page says today. Nothing changed when this
went in.

## What you can change

**The secret** — the introduction and the three steps explaining how the
result reaches you without reaching the parents. This is the part of the
service that is unlike anything else you sell.

**What is included** — the six entries.

**How the moment happens** — the introduction and the four points.

**The colour themes** — three of them, each with an id that travels with the
enquiry. Add or reword them. A theme missing its id or its label is dropped
rather than rendered, because a choice the enquiry cannot name is worse than
no choice.

**Decoration designs** — empty, same as the proposals page. Add one with a
photograph and it becomes a real selectable design.

**The sequence** — the seven steps, in order.

**Photographs and video** — the heading, what the set covers, how they arrive,
and the two "we do not publish a number / a deadline" paragraphs.

**Practical** — how long it takes, how much notice, who it is for, and the
maternity cross-sell.

## Three things to be careful with

**Do not turn "how the moment happens" into a list of props.** What pops,
sprays, burns or drifts is the venue's call and changes by date and by wind.
A named effect on this page is how somebody arrives expecting something that
was never possible. The page currently explains how the decision gets made
instead, which is both true and reassuring.

**The photograph at the top is one real reveal you ran.** The note beside it
says that it shows the decoration from one evening, not a design anyone can
order by name. Keep that distinction — the picture is persuasive enough to be
read as a catalogue if the words let it.

**There is no price for this service, and no payment terms at all.** Not an
unpublished figure: nothing has ever been agreed. The proposal's 25/75 split
was supplied for the proposal and does not transfer here. If you set terms for
a gender reveal, **tell me rather than typing them into the quotation box** —
the booking flow has to agree with them.

## The limit of the safety net

Worth knowing now that you are editing live copy. The automated checks read
the **code**. They will stop a price appearing in the fallback text, and they
stop fields being silently ignored. They cannot see what you type into the
Studio. If you put a figure in the quotation box, it goes on the site and no
check will catch it.

That is not a reason to be nervous about editing — it is a reason to tell me
when you set a price, so the rest of the site can agree with it.
