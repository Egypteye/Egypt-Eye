# The content map

Per-article specification for the clusters that can be produced now.
20 November 2026. **Nothing here is published.** For approval.

---

## How to read this

Each cluster states its **defaults once** — commercial page, photography
source, CTA — then lists only what varies per article. Repeating eleven
identical fields forty times would hide the differences that matter.

**On keywords.** I have no keyword tool, so these are *target* keywords chosen
on intent and on how a customer phrases the question, not on measured volume.
Egypt Eye's own articles declare targets the same way. Treat them as the
editorial brief, not as a demand forecast, until we have Search Console.

**Priority** is P1 (first batch), P2 (next), P3 (once the cluster has proved
itself).

**Photography** cites the Drive folder. Exact per-image selection, with SEO
filenames and alt text, comes after you approve the architecture — picking 150
individual frames before the article list is agreed would be work thrown away.
