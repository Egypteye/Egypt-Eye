## 6. Where the research went

Two findings shaped the page, and both are in the code with their sources.

**Your rate is competitive and the page leads with it.** Commercial rooftop
location hire runs roughly $105 to $370 an hour on the US marketplaces, with
platform averages near $306. $150 an hour with no minimum sits at the bottom of
that band, for a backdrop none of them has.

**The stronger argument is access, not money.** Shooting a commercial inside the
Giza archaeological complex is reported by production fixers at around $3,000 a
day, on a permit that takes two to four weeks through the Ministry. Your roof is
private property outside the complex. A brand gets the skyline without the
permit, the wait or the day rate. That is now a section on the page.

Those figures are context for deciding how to present your own rate. None of
them is quoted to a visitor as a competitor's price.
